Movement lab

Chip Stack Movement Lab

A first-person physics controller rebuilt from the shipped movement values: walk 8, sprint 12 and crouch 5 m/s, a +11 m/s jump, 30 m/s² surface-hugging gravity, and auto step-up to 0.5 m. The course has labeled steps, ramps and platforms so you can check each limit.

The game tries to lock your mouse for looking around. If it can't, drag with the left button. Esc releases the mouse.
